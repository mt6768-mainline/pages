Mainline Linux, U-Boot, and TF-A support for MediaTek MT6768 platform.

== Before We Start
If you already own one of the <<_supported_devices,supported devices>> with matching configuration, you can skip porting process. You don't need to modify the kernel or U-Boot, and can simply build and flash the existing configurations. Yet making backups and collecting data from Android is still helpful if you want to contribute.

If you are *porting a new device* or *adding hardware support*, keep the following in mind:

* Some I2C, SPI and DSI devices lack upstream support, so you may need to write drivers for them yourself.
* We replace vendor firmware with custom *TF-A* and *U-Boot* to avoid broken vendor blobs and DTB pollution. Shim bootloaders (`uniLoader`, U-Boot chainloaded as a kernel) will not be merged into the Linux tree without good reason.
* While you can debug many kernel issues over USB gadget serial or `simplefb`/`pstore` once U-Boot is running, those tools cannot help if TF-A or early U-Boot fails to initialize. Furthermore, if the board hangs hard during early bringup, the only way to forcefully reset is by disconnecting the battery flex cable. For initial device bringup, being comfortable opening the phone and wiring up UART is strongly recommended. Don't blame me for killing your device later.

== Prerequisites
Before starting a new port, make sure you have:

1. A tested way to read/write partitions (`mtkclient`, `penumbra`, or others) even when TF-A, LK/U-Boot, and Linux are completely broken.
2. (strongly recommended) An UART dongle with 1.8V logic level, such as FT2232H or FT232H. CP2102 or CH341 may work on certain boards.
3. Host with Linux or WSL (untested) with basic knowledge of C, devicetrees, and compiling kernels.

[CAUTION]
====
If BootROM or Preloader download mode is unavailable, or there's no signed DA, attempting mainline or even a shim bootloader is a *TERRIBLE* idea. If stock LK becomes unhappy with your DTB or boot image, it will bootloop with no way to interrupt it besides flashing a working image via download mode.

*Do NOT use Android kernel LPE exploits to flash firmware or mainline images.* While LPE rooting is popular on locked devices, it relies on Android booting. dm-verity will still brick your device.
====

== Supported devices
To identify your device you either need to take UART log dump or take a look at stock `/proc/cmdline` contents.

=== Xiaomi Redmi Note 9
* Panel: `LCM_name=nt36672A_fhdp_dsi_vdo_tianma_lcm_drv` -> `tianma,tl065fvxm03-01`
* Backlight: `:bklic=24` -> `kinetic,ktd3137`

If your hardware matches the above, the device is fully supported under `mt6768-xiaomi-merlin-nt36672a-ktd3137.dtb` configuration. If your unit shipped with a different panel or backlight revision, you will need to create overlay for your variant.
