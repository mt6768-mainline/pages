Mainline Linux, U-Boot, and TF-A support for MediaTek MT6768 platform.

WARNING: *YOUR WARRANTY IS VOID.* You are solely responsible for any damage, data loss, or permanent bricks caused to your hardware.

== Before we start
Before attempting a port, understand what you are getting into:

* Some I2C and SPI devices may be unsupported in the upstream. You will need to write drivers for them.

* We replace vendor firmware with custom *TF-A* and *U-Boot* to avoid dealing with crap vendor blobs. Shim bootloaders (uniLoader, U-Boot chainloaded as a kernel) won't be merged into the Linux tree without an exceptional hardware reason.

* You can't debug TF-A or U-Boot with `pstore` or `simplefb`. Stop here and don't waste time if you fear opening the device.

== Prerequisites

Do not start porting unless you have *all* of the following:

1. A tested way to read/write partitions (`mtkclient`, `penumbra`, or others) even when TF-A, LK/U-Boot, and Linux are completely broken.
2. An UART dongle with 1.8V logic level, such as FT2232H or FT232H.
3. Host with Linux or WSL (untested) with basic knowledge of C, devicetrees, and compiling kernels.

[CAUTION]
====
If BootROM or Preloader download mode is unavailable, or there's no signed DA, attempting mainline or even a shim bootloader is a *TERRIBLE* idea. If stock LK becomes unhappy with your DTB or boot image, it will bootloop with no way to interrupt it besides flashing a working image via download mode.

*Do NOT use Android kernel LPE exploits to flash firmware or mainline images.* While LPE rooting is popular on locked devices, it relies on Android booting. dm-verity will still brick your device.
====
